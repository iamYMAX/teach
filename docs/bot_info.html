Telegram Bot Information

Our Telegram bot provides interactive access to course materials and tests.

How to Connect:

  1. Open Telegram.
  2. Search for our bot (Bot name will be here).
  3. Start the bot and follow the instructions.

Available Commands:

  • /start - Welcome message and instructions.
  • /courses - List available courses.
  • /lesson <number> - Get theory and practice for a lesson.
  • /test <number> - Take a test for a lesson.
  • /help - Show help message.
